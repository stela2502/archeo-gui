No commercial license is required for uses permitted by the PolyForm Noncommercial License, including:

* teaching and education,
* use by educational institutions,
* academic and public research,
* personal research and experimentation,
* non-commercial scientific projects,
* use by charitable and other qualifying non-commercial organizations.

This is intended to keep `archeo-gui` freely available to researchers, students, teachers, universities, and the broader scientific community.

## Commercial use

Commercial use requires a separate commercial license.

Examples include use of `archeo-gui` by or on behalf of a commercial company as part of its business activities, including internal research and development, commercial data analysis, product development, consulting, or commercial services.

A commercial license may cover, depending on the agreement:

* internal company use,
* multiple users or installations,
* commercial research and development,
* consulting and analytical services,
* integration into commercial workflows,
* redistribution or bundling,
* OEM use,
* hosted or managed services.

Commercial terms can be adapted to the intended use.

## Why dual licensing?

The aim is simple:

**Scientists should be able to use the software for science and teaching without paying for it. Companies that derive commercial value from it should contribute financially to its development.**

Commercial licensing helps support continued maintenance and development while keeping the software accessible to academic users.
